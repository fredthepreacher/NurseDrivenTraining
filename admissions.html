Admissions & Enrollment

Here's exactly what to expect, from your first phone call to your first day of class.

What Happens After You Contact Us

Five simple steps to get started

Confirm your program

We confirm whether CNA, HHA, or CPR is the right fit for your goals.

Review schedule & start date

We walk through the next available start date and class-day options.

Answer pricing questions

We explain tuition, the $25 registration fee, and payment options.

Walk through enrollment

We guide you through the steps to officially register for your program.

Prepare for Day 1

We tell you what to bring and what to expect before your first class.

Class Schedule

  • Classes are held three days per week: Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday
  • Morning class: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Evening class: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM
  • Saturday clinicals: 10:00 AM–1:00 PM or 2:00–5:00 PM
