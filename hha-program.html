Home Health Aide (HHA) Program

Gain the skills and knowledge needed to deliver compassionate, in-home care to clients in a variety of settings.

4–6 Class Hours Scheduled with your trainer $240

What you'll learn

This program focuses on assisting individuals with daily living activities, maintaining a safe and supportive home environment, and promoting clients' comfort, independence, and well-being.

  • Assisting with activities of daily living
  • Maintaining a safe, supportive home environment
  • Supporting client comfort and independence
  • Communication and professionalism in home-care settings

The HHA Program is ideal for individuals seeking a rewarding caregiving role, or CNAs wishing to expand their skills within the healthcare field.

Program at a Glance

  • Length: 4–6 total class hours
  • Scheduling: Offered based on availability, scheduled directly with your trainer
  • Format: Personalized training plan built around your timeline
  • Tuition: $240 + $25 registration fee
