CNA Training Program

Our 6-Week CNA Training Program prepares students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to excel as Certified Nursing Assistants.

6 Weeks Wed / Fri / Sat $1,275

What you'll learn

Through a combination of classroom instruction, hands-on skills practice, and real-world clinical experience, students learn essential caregiving techniques, patient safety, communication skills, and professional standards of care.

  • Essential patient care & caregiving techniques
  • Patient safety and infection control basics
  • Communication skills for clinical settings
  • Professional standards of care
  • Hands-on skills practice with instructor feedback
  • Real-world clinical experience

Upon completing the program, graduates are eligible to sit for the Florida state CNA certification exam. Course completion prepares you for the exam; it does not guarantee a passing score, state certification, or employment.

Program at a Glance

  • Length: As little as 6 weeks (6–8 hrs/week)
  • Class days: Wednesday, Friday, Saturday
  • Morning class: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Evening class: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM
  • Saturday clinicals: 10 AM–1 PM or 2–5 PM
  • Tuition: $1,275 + $25 registration fee
