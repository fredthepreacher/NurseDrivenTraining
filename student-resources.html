Student Resources

Tools and guidance to help you feel prepared before, during, and after your program.

Before Class Starts

  • Bring a valid photo ID on Day 1
  • Wear comfortable clothing for hands-on practice
  • Review your class schedule and arrive a few minutes early

During Your Program

  • Ask questions — small class sizes mean real instructor time
  • Practice skills outside of class when possible
  • Reach out to your trainer if a session needs to be rescheduled

Preparing for Your State Exam

  • Review patient care fundamentals and safety procedures
  • Practice hands-on skills until they feel automatic
  • Ask your trainer about exam-day logistics and what to expect
